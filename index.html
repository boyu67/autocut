AutoCUT

AutoCUT 是個人使用的影片剪輯自動化工具：從指定的 Google 雲端硬碟資料夾下載直播錄影，在本機剪成精華影片，再把成品上傳到指定資料夾。

AutoCUT is a personal video-editing automation tool. It downloads stream recordings from one designated Google Drive folder, edits highlight videos on a local computer, and uploads the results to another designated folder.

隱私權政策 Privacy Policy

最後更新：2026-11-03

  • AutoCUT 只讀取授權帳號雲端硬碟中「黃狗毛片上傳」資料夾內的檔案，並在「黃狗精華成品」資料夾建立檔案。不讀取、不修改其他檔案。
  • 下載的影片只在本機處理，不提供給任何第三方，也不用於廣告或其他用途。
  • 授權憑證只存放在執行 AutoCUT 的電腦上。隨時可以到 Google 帳戶的「第三方存取權」移除授權。
  • 處理完成的原始錄影會在本機空間不足時自動刪除。

AutoCUT only reads files inside the designated upload folder and creates files inside the designated output folder of the authorizing account. Downloaded videos are processed locally and are never shared with third parties or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored only on the computer running AutoCUT and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account third-party access page. Local copies of recordings are deleted when disk space is needed.
